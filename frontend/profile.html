💡 Smart Soil & Water Management for Farmers

💧

Precision Irrigation

When soil is Dry, apply water through drip tubes at the root zone to save up to 50% water compared to flood irrigation.

🍂

Organic Mulching

Spread straw or crop residue across the soil bed. This prevents moisture evaporation in hot weather and nourishes soil microbes.

🚜

Soil Aeration & Drainage

When soil is Wet or Clayey, maintain clean drainage furrows. Aeration stimulates aerobic bacteria and prevents root rot.
