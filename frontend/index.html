Understanding Soil Moisture States

Maintaining the right moisture balance is critical for crop growth and water conservation.

Low Moisture
☀️

Dry Soil

Soil has cracked or powdery texture with minimal capillary water.

  • ⚠️ Roots cannot absorb nitrogen or phosphorus
  • ⚠️ Plants begin wilting and slowing cell division
  • Irrigation Action: Irrigate promptly via drip or furrow watering to restore moisture.
Optimal Zone
🌱

Moderate Soil

Dark, crumbly, and cohesive soil with ideal pore space for air and water.

  • ✅ Maximum nutrient absorption and root vitality
  • ✅ Healthy soil microbes decompose organic matter
  • Irrigation Action: No watering required today. Maintain mulching to lock moisture.
Saturated
💧

Wet Soil

Waterlogged or muddy soil with pore spaces completely filled with water.

  • ⚠️ Lack of oxygen suffocates crop root systems
  • ⚠️ Elevated risk of fungal damping-off and root rot
  • Irrigation Action: Pause irrigation immediately and clear surface drainage ditches.
